Why

AI services need open, per-use payments

The problemAI and GPU capacity are sold through closed accounts, monthly plans and card payments. Agents and small providers cannot pay or be paid per request, and buyers cannot check who they are paying.
ARLOne token on Base for AI and compute: pay per request over x402, find services in an open registry, hire them through an escrow, and rate them after paying.
  • Built on open standards, not a closed platform
  • Low fees and fast settlement on Base, an Ethereum L2

Product

Open standards around one token

x402 paymentsAI services paid per use in ARL, settled with Permit2 (@arl/payments).
ERC-8004 registryService identity and reputation, rated by paying clients.
ERC-8183 escrowJobs paid into escrow and released on delivery (ARLJobs).
ComputeGPU and CPU capacity paid per second.
StakingSynthetix-style staking rewards (ARLStakingRewards).
ZK signalsAnonymous polls with Semaphore and Noir proofs.

Status: contracts and packages written and tested; token system live on Base Sepolia. Services are not live yet.
