An AI job's cost isn't known until it runs. So why pay a fixed price?

This is the idea behind ARL's "Payment Rail First" strategy: build the way compute gets paid before anything else.

How it works:
→ The payer signs a ceiling: the maximum the job may cost.
→ The service meters actual usage, for example seconds of GPU time.
→ A facilitator settles the metered amount on-chain. Never more than the ceiling.

Under the hood: the x402 "upto" scheme and Permit2 on Base. The signature covers the payee, the facilitator, the token, the ceiling, a one-time nonce and a deadline. A replayed or tampered payment is refused.

ARL itself: 21,000,000 fixed supply. No mint function, no owner, no upgrade path.

• No audit of ARL's contracts yet.

We build in public. The code is open source on GitHub (link in bio).
